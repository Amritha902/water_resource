# Reproduction report — both base papers

Generated by `experiments/18_write_reproduction.py` from `results/`. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. The shared plant

BSM1 re-implemented from its published definition: ASM1 (13 components, 8 processes) at 15 °C, a ten-layer Takács clarifier, five reactors (1 000/1 000/1 333/1 333/1 333 m³), `Q_r = 18 446`, `Q_w = 385 m³/d`, explicit integration with four sub-steps per 45 s period. `tests/test_bsm1.py` checks the open-loop steady state against the published values (`S_O,5 ≈ 0.49`, `S_NO,2 ≈ 3.66`, effluent TSS ≈ 12.5 g/m³), non-negativity, settler thickening > 50× and both actuator gain signs. Influent: dry/rain/storm generated from the documented BSM1 statistics with the pollutograph sharpened so the discharge limits bind; statistically equivalent, not the official files. **Consequence: every claim is a comparison between methods on the same simulator; absolute values are not expected to equal the papers' tables.**

### 2.2 Deviations from the printed paper

| # | Paper | This code | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | critic descends eq. (15) in the weights producing `Q(x_k)` | TD(0): correct `Q(x_{k−1})` towards `U_k + λQ(x_k)` | the printed direction diverges (§2.4) |
| 2 | actor gradient via `∂Q/∂a` (eq. 27) | `∂Q/∂Δa + ∂Q/∂a` | `a_k = a_{k−1} + Δa_k`: both critic inputs depend on the actor output |
| 3 | no bias stated | bias input on the **critic** only | a tanh critic without bias is 0 at the set point and cannot represent `Q > 0` there; the **actor** has none, so `Δa = 0` at zero error, as eq. (8) requires |
| 4 | raw units, scale in `W, Y, R, P` | inputs and effort terms normalised by actuator range | equivalent re-parameterisation; lets both agents share learning rates |
| 5 | `a` unbounded | `a` clipped to ±10 % of each range (±24 1/d, ±9 223 m³/d); `Δa = Δa_max·tanh(·)` with 4 1/d, 1 500 m³/d per step | keeps `a` a correction to the prior |
| 6 | plain gradient steps | actor outer-weight decay 2.0, critic 1e−4, gradient norm clip 10 | without decay a start-up transient latched the actor at its tanh rail |
| 7 | `W, Y, R, P`, network sizes not all stated | `W=1, P=0.25, Y=0.02, R=0.05`, critic 16 hidden, actor 12, `l_c=0.02`, `l_a=0.002` | chosen by us |
| 8 | official BSM1 code and influent files | re-implementation; statistically equivalent influent | files not redistributable — **absolute values differ from Table II** |
| 9 | — | 1-day PID warm-up; first day of each run excluded from metrics | every controller starts from the same plant state |
| 10 | (`MAACC-tuned`, §2.4) | 10× increment and authority (40 1/d, 15 000 m³/d per step; ±240, ±92 230), no actor decay, `W=30, P=1, Y=0.02, R=0`, `l_c=0.05`, `l_a=0.01` | the conservative defaults above stay at PID level; these reach the paper's gains. 20-seed search on dry weather |
| 11 | — (`MAACC-tuned`) | **safety fallback**: if the 1-h mean of `|z_i|/z_scale` exceeds 0.15 or of `|tanh|` exceeds 0.7, the actor is zeroed, its `a` is handed to the PID (bumpless) and it waits 1 h | without it 8/20 runs diverge: the untrained critic's slope sends the actor to its rail within ~40 steps |
| 12 | — (`MAACC-tuned`) | 5 fallbacks within a day re-draw the critic's outer weights | a critic that diverged mid-run kept re-teaching a bad policy |

Two earlier deviations were **removed** because they broke the incremental policy: a bias input on the actor (so `π(0) ≠ 0` and `a` drifted steadily into its clip) and a leaky integrator `a_k = 0.999·a_{k−1} + Δa_k`. With them, the learned term sat at its clip 92 % of the run and acted as a constant offset; without them it moves with the tracking error (§2.5).

### 2.5 Correction: eq. (15) diverges

Eq. (15) is `e_k = U_k + λQ(x_k) − Q(x_{k−1})` and eq. (18) descends ½e² in the weights β of `Q(x_k) = βᵀϑ`; the gradient is `e·λ·ϑ`. For a slowly varying state (`Q_k ≈ Q_{k−1} = Q`):

```
Q  ←  Q · (1 + l_c λ (1 − λ) ‖ϑ‖²)  −  l_c λ ‖ϑ‖² U
```

The multiplier exceeds 1 for every `l_c > 0`.  Theorem 1 (UUB of the weight error) is not contradicted — a bound can be large; its `l_c` condition is a one-step no-overshoot condition, not a contraction.

### 2.6 What the learned term does

A learning-free control, `experiments/17_maacc_offset_control.py`, separates learning from re-tuning: the PID prior plus a *constant* −9 223 m³/d on `Q_a`, and the PID prior with its integrator clamp lowered to −9 223. IAE `S_NO,2`, noisy condition, mean of seeds:

| weather | PID | MAACC | PID + constant offset | PID, lowered clamp |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dry | 0.0832 | 0.0607 | 0.0590 | 0.0604 |
| rain | 0.0797 | 0.0627 | 0.0614 | 0.0591 |
| storm | 0.1214 | 0.1092 | 0.1110 | 0.1018 |

Part of what the agent learns on the recycle loop is a slowly varying negative correction that compensates the PID prior's anti-windup at zero recycle; a better-tuned prior gets that for free. Read MAACC's gain against both columns, not against the PID alone.

### 3.5 Corrections needed for the published recipe to learn

As published, the agent collapses to zero aeration (`--published-env` keeps that environment):

1. **The observation is not Markov** — with an incremental action the actuator is a hidden integrator. Add `KLa_5/240` to the state and use an absolute action.
2. **45 s decisions with γ = 0.99 give a ~75 min horizon**, but effluent NH₄ responds over hours. Hold each action 15 min.
3. **Value scale** — the critic learns values ~−250 from rewards ~−2.5 and `∂Q/∂a` has the wrong sign for weeks. Scale the reward by `1 − γ`; actor weight decay 1e−4 and a 1e−3 pre-tanh penalty.

Further deviations: AE enters the reward as `KLa_5/240` (the paper does not give units; raw AE ≈ 3 500 would swamp the 0.42 term — affine, so the same objective up to scale); OU σ annealed 0.35 → 0.05, buffer 80 000; 20 passes over the training week instead of 7 days.

### 3.6 Reproducibility

Re-training DDPG-B and PANDA-RL (dry, seed 0) in a fresh session with the benchmark's settings (`--threads 2`) gives the committed numbers exactly. With a different thread count the first training week still matches, but floating-point summation order differs and training diverges — to a different point on the same energy/violation trade-off.
